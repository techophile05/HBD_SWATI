===================================================================
  🎂 HOW TO ADD MORE PHOTOS TO SWATI'S BIRTHDAY SLIDESHOW 📸
===================================================================

You have 3 super easy ways to add more photos:

METHOD 1: DRAG & DROP DIRECTLY (Easiest & Fastest!)
--------------------------------------------------
1. Open "index.html" in your web browser.
2. Scroll down to the "Magical Gallery" section.
3. Simply drag any images (.jpg, .jpeg, .png, .webp) from your
   folder or desktop and drop them right onto the slideshow!
4. They appear immediately and stay saved even when you refresh.

METHOD 2: DOUBLE-CLICK "update-images.bat"
-----------------------------------------
1. Drop any new photos into this folder (or the "assets" folder).
2. Double-click "update-images.bat".
3. It automatically updates "images-list.js" in 1 second.
4. Refresh index.html in your browser!

METHOD 3: AUTOMATIC NAMING CONVENTION
------------------------------------
If you drop photos named:
  photo1.jpg, photo2.jpg, photo3.jpg ...
  OR image1.jpg, image2.jpg ...
  OR 1.jpg, 2.jpg, 3.jpg ...
  OR swati1.jpg, swati2.jpg ...
The slideshow will automatically detect and display them without editing any files!

===================================================================
  🎂 BIRTHDAY CAKE CUTTING CEREMONY INSTRUCTIONS 🍰
===================================================================
1. Choose between 4 animated cake flavors (Rose Velvet, Chocolate, Lavender, Strawberry).
2. Click "🕯️ Blow Candles" to make a wish and extinguish the candle flames.
3. Click any slice on the cake (or click "🔪 Cut Next Slice")!
4. An animated golden knife slices down with sound effects.
5. The slice slides out onto a dessert plate and opens a heartfelt birthday message card!
6. Cut all 6 slices for the grand celebration fanfare!
